# Required interaction and visual checks

- Left mouse drag rotates immediately, with unrestricted horizontal azimuth and bounded vertical tilt. Right mouse drag pans in screen space. No Orbit toggle. Never impose a wrapped azimuth range; verify both horizontal and vertical rotation. Do not clamp target axes during panning. Wheel zooms. Touch: one finger pans, two fingers zoom/orbit. Suppress the canvas context menu. Keep a visible control hint and working Fit office action.
- Working bots type with both hands above a keyboard, elbows bent and tiny alternating taps. Never animate a large shoulder/arm pumping motion near the lap. Verify keyboard alignment at ordinary and tall desks close up, over time.
- Idle bots stay still. Normal journeys never teleport. Do not invent live handoffs.
- Floor planes must not share the slab's top depth. Keep native resolution and matte floor materials. Visually pan and orbit under each lighting preset.
- Now uses device local time; explicit Day/Evening/Night modes persist. Never let camera controls change lighting.
- Check labels, selection, tooltips, narrow layouts, login cancellation, and camera reset. Use actual label bounds for collision avoidance.
- Run the production build and inspect the actual UI after changes. Record unverified checks honestly; a successful build alone is not visual approval.
- Preserve secrets and the Cloudflare backend. Push only the authorized redesign branch; never merge automatically.

- Public simulation personas are separate from LIVE_TEAM. Never change real IDs or server roster for a demo request. Demo brands are illustrative, not integrations; keep that disclosure visible. Update department carpets, tags, sidebar and role-specific status lines together.
- Marketing footage must visibly disclose simulation, use actual app renders, and avoid fabricated performance, autonomous execution or integration claims. Never upload or post without explicit authorization.

- Marketing exports: reject source frame gaps over 350 ms; never blend across gaps or text cuts. Inspect the entire timeline and every transition in both aspect ratios. Do not describe decoding checks or sampled stills as an audiovisual playback review. Keep frontend/technology-stack exposition out of promotional narration and copy.
